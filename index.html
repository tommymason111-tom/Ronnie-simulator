How to play Grab: grip or trigger (or pinch with hand tracking).
Eat: bring food to your mouth. Each touch = one bite.
Drink MONSTOR: bring the can to your mouth and tip it back past 60° → MANGO LOCO MODE (x3 Chonk for 10s).
Throw: let go mid-swing. Hit Tommy for a reaction.
Broccoli! When Tommy winds up, swat it away with a fast hand swing — or catch it and throw it back.
Move: left stick. Turn: right stick (30° snap).
Chonk Meter: look at your left wrist.
Grab anyone: grip or pinch on any part of Tommy or the squad and fling them about. Slap with a fast swing.
Game modes: pick one on the start screen, or press a button on the GAME MODES board in the level.
The squad: Chloe names your food (guilt bonus), Lewis swaps in protein shakes, Harry "throws food away" (catch it in your mouth), Aidan brings a pint (Stout Mode), Noah tackles, Oliver films you, Dylan ambushes from the bin (shake him off), Connor buys a round of stout, Ethan curls Galáctico free kicks of veg at you.
No headset (phone / tablet / computer)? Press PLAY ON THIS SCREEN. Left joystick (or WASD) to walk, drag to look, tap food to pick it up, EAT / THROW buttons (or E / Space), tap broccoli to swat it, tap Tommy to slap him.
